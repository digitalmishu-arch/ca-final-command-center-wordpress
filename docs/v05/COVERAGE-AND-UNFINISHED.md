The connected workbook catalogue contained **79 FR PDFs**: 17 RTP, 23 MTP question papers, 23 MTP answers and 16 suggested-answer books. All 79 were retrieved/reused with SHA-256 checkpoints and passed through the local extraction worker: **1,776 pages**. PDF count is not question count.

The private delivery's `coverage-report.json`, `manifest.json`, `source-inventory.json`, `duplicate-exceptions.json` and `exceptions.json` are authoritative for final counts. Candidate parents and separately classifiable subparts are counted separately. A suggested-answer book may contain both the exam question and its solution; this does not mean its separate examination-paper PDF was processed.

The Drive FR subtree additionally lists **82** mostly teacher/book/note PDFs whose official publisher identity or separate reproduction rights are unconfirmed. They are metadata-only exceptions. ICAI permission does not automatically cover another author's original material.

Three official May 2025/2026/2027 study listings were reconciled separately. Their 15 module initial-pages PDFs yielded numbered contents evidence and **3,069 proposed official hierarchy nodes**, including 17 chapters per edition and detailed numbered topics/subtopics. These are draft source transcriptions, not automatically reviewed headings. Fifteen ambiguous entries/parents remain explicit exceptions. Website/contents wording differences are preserved as evidence rather than overwriting existing approved titles. Initial-pages contents extraction is not complete extraction of all chapter illustrations/practice questions.

The additional official-resource manifest inventories study PDFs and six newer actual examination-paper PDFs plus an additional answer book. The web reader exposes some text/contents but no raw layout bytes for these sources; a direct ICAI CDN download probe returned HTTP 403. Each outstanding URL has an explicit action to supply the original privately. This is **not** a claim that every URL individually returned 403, nor that the complete external historical examination/study archive was exhaustively discovered. Older official historical source/applicability research remains open.
